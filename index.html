About Me

Hi there! I'm a CS student who likes building at the layers people don't always see: interpreters, emulators, renderers, assemblers.

I've written a functional math language in Rust, a CHIP-8 emulator in C, and a ray tracer in C++. I also designed a small CPU with a friend; he built it in Logisim and I wrote the assembler.

My most-used project is a tool that converts brushes between painting apps, because I draw too and wanted to keep my brushes when switching software.
